3.3 Data Preview 2
Data Preview 2 (DP2) will provide the first large-area, multi-band dataset generated from a
subset of science-grade astronomically useful images taken during the LSSTCam on-sky commissioning campaign, (§ 2.3) and early operations. The full definition and production plan for
DP2 is described in RTN-111.
As of April 2026, the DP2 processing is still ongoing. A temporary tutorial notebook to explore
and visualize the preliminary DP2 visit and tract metadata (e.g., coordinates, filters, dates) will
be available in the Rubin Science Platform, under the ”Tutorials - DP2” drop-down menu.
The planned release time range for DP2 is July – September 2026.
